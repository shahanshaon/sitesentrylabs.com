Our Story

From a shared vision to industry leadership

2022

The Beginning

SiteSentryLabs was founded by Shahan Shoan and Faiaz Rahaman, who identified a critical gap in the market: web development agencies neglecting security, and security firms lacking development expertise.

2023

First Major Breakthrough

We developed our proprietary Secure Development Framework (SDF), integrating security at every stage of the development lifecycle. Our first enterprise client reported zero security breaches in their first year.

2024

Expansion & Recognition

Expanded our team to include specialized cybersecurity experts and web developers. Recognized as "Most Innovative Security Startup" by TechSecurity Magazine.

2025

Industry Leadership

Launched our DevSecOps consultancy division. Partnered with major cloud providers to develop industry-leading security protocols for cloud-native applications.

2026

Today & Beyond

Now serving clients across three continents with a team of 9 experts. Continuously evolving to combat emerging threats in an increasingly complex digital landscape.
