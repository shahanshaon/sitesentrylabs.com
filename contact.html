Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services and process

What is your typical response time for security incidents?

Our Security Operations Center provides 24/7 monitoring with an average response time of under 15 minutes for critical security incidents. We follow a tiered response system based on severity levels.

Do you offer ongoing support after web development projects?

Yes, we offer various maintenance and support packages. Our standard package includes security updates, performance monitoring, and 24/7 technical support. Custom packages are available based on your specific needs.

What compliance standards do you help with?

We assist with GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, PCI DSS, ISO 27001, SOC 2, and other industry-specific compliance requirements. Our team includes legal and compliance experts to ensure comprehensive coverage.

How do you ensure the security of your own development process?

We follow a DevSecOps approach with security integrated at every stage. This includes secure coding practices, automated security testing, code reviews, and regular penetration testing of our own infrastructure and tools.
